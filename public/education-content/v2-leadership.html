Leadership mechanics for launches

  • CEO sets macro WHAT + WHY; leaders write the HOW so they own it.
  • Extra resource request must answer: does this make the goal timeline faster? Honest trade.
  • First 90 days: moments that matter → team writes SOPs; raise the floor without lowering the ceiling.
  • Middle-manager incentives: upsell / cross-sell / referral / return POC; 100% NRR month bonus.
  • Reality check: one service line’s SAM has diminishing returns — new divisions exist to open new SAMs.

Brandastic

Justin holds WHAT/WHY on any new offer. Samantha/Sandy/Melissa own HOW in their lanes. No hire of a new middle manager until account/strategist trigger is hit.
