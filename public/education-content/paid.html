Ads only as backup

We are good at ads. We will keep running them for clients and sometimes for ourselves. But Brandastic will not try to buy all growth with ads alone.

What this means day to day

  • Sam keeps owning paid craft for clients.
  • Brandastic new-business ads get their own budget line and a monthly cap Justin sets.
  • We aim ads at warm people when we can: event guests, website readers, past proposals.
  • Every month we ask: how many meetings came from ads vs nights/gifts/referrals/website?

Step-by-step

Split the reporting

Sam Separate “client ad work” from “Brandastic new-business ads” so we stop mixing them.

Set the cap

Justin Write the monthly max for Brandastic new-business ads.

Build warm audiences

Sam Event RSVPs, site visitors to hub pages, past proposal emails.

Weekly cleanup

Kill weak ads. Keep hooks short and clear.

Done looks like this

Two budget views exist, cap is written down, warm audiences exist, one monthly source-of-meetings review has happened.

This is a working handbook. Each page should be clear enough that someone can do the job without a meeting.
