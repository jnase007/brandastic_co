Video 02 · When to launch

When to launch (and when not to)

Launch when you can incubate a real P&L path. Do not launch because a client asked once.

Their decision frame

They abandoned cheap M&A. Internal launch won when they could staff a minimum viable department and take a service to real revenue (they cited ~$3M P&L in under 12 months historically).

Launch when: you can win best-in-category RFPs; you have research-backed POV; you can staff MVD without starving core delivery; GP path exists beyond cross-sell only.

Do not launch when: only attach revenue; no expert bench; it is a one-off client request outside ICP; you would need a middle manager before you have ~5 accounts/strategists of proof.

Brandastic

Map every service we sell today against “could we win the best RFP?” Anything that fails becomes partner/referral, not a fake menu item. New launches (if any) follow the 14-step build list on the V2 hub.
