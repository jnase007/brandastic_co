Annual Bonus Program

Plain-English read of Directive’s Christmas bonus calculator. This is a tenure-based holiday check, not a performance-review score formula.

Source: Christmas Bonus Calculator

What it’s based on (and what it’s not)

  • Based on: how long you’ve been employed (start / anniversary date) + a monthly bonus rate that rises with tenure years.
  • Not based on: individual performance reviews, accounts managed, or billable hours in this spreadsheet.
  • When paid: labeled as a December 15 check.

So: Christmas / year-end tenure bonus calculator — separate from the AE commission plan and separate from performance management docs.

Eligibility rules (exact from the sheet)

  • Paid on the last payroll before Christmas break (sheet also notes Dec 15 check in the math area)
  • Must be here at least a year to be eligible
  • Anniversary = full-time employment start date
  • Interns, part-time, and contractors are not eligible

How the payout is calculated

The sheet has two tabs: Payout Template (the math for one person) and Data (lookup table of rates by tenure year).

1) Inputs

  • Start / anniversary date (drives how many full months and leftover days this year count)
  • Two monthly rate amounts (for the two tenure bands the person sits in during the year — “year multiplier 1” and “year multiplier 2”)

Month-by-month view (Payout Template): Jan–Dec each get a monthly amount. If that month is before the anniversary month → lower tenure rate; if after → higher tenure rate; anniversary month uses the crossover/day pro-rate. Year total = sum of the 12 months.

2) Full months

  • Counts months before the anniversary month in the year
  • Counts months after
  • Multiplies each block by the matching monthly rate
  • Adds those month blocks together

Example structure from the template formulas:

  • Months band 1 × monthly rate 1
  • Months band 2 × monthly rate 2
  • Sum = months portion of bonus

3) Crossover / partial month (days)

  • If the anniversary falls mid-month, leftover days in that month are pro-rated
  • Days before anniversary use rate 1; days after use rate 2
  • Day payout = (monthly rate ÷ total days in window) × days in that slice

4) Total payout

  • Total = months portion + days portion
  • Note on sheet: payout on the December 15 check

The rate table (Data tab)

Rates step up with years. The payout template pulls the right rate(s) for the person’s tenure during the year (including the step-up at anniversary).

Worked idea (from template numbers)

  • Months band totals example: 500 + 1,230 = 1,730
  • Days band totals example: ~93.33 + ~13.67 = ~107
  • Total payout example: 1,730 + 107 = 1,837 on the Dec 15 check

Exact dollars depend on the person’s start date and which tenure rates apply.

Brandastic takeaway

  • If we copy the idea: publish a simple tenure table + Dec payout date before the year starts.
  • Don’t mix this with review ratings unless we deliberately design a second bonus.
  • Keep sales commission separate (already on the AE/SME pages).
