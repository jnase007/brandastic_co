Customer marketing & account expansion (their system)

Hire SaaS-style customer marketing talent. Build motions that create referrals, reopens, and expansion without buying every booking with ads.

1. Motions that drove bookings

  • Past POCs + referrals — major booking source (~$7M bookings last year via referrals + returning contacts in their telling)
  • Hard 30-day push example: 4 meetings booked
  • Trigger alerts on job changes, capital raises, other buying signals — act fast
  • Job-change champagne / gift when a contact lands a new role
  • Team bonuses for referrals
  • Stay in relationship even when the founder is not the day-to-day face

2. Account expansion stack

  • Tool they named: Stratosphere — sentiment across Gong + email + calls + Slack
  • Happiness / unhappiness visibility for leadership
  • White-space mapping per client (what they wanted in sales but could not fund yet)
  • Account directors (~26 accounts in their model): happy + results first, then expand white space with a POV (why us vs internal)
  • Rule of thumb: hit client goals before asking for growth intros

3. Loss / offboarding accountability

  • Public client offboarding channel
  • Must post AI prompt + AI root-cause output (no sugarcoating)
  • Can add context; cannot delete or obfuscate
  • Goal: culture of truth so the org actually improves

4. Sales call discipline (when leadership is on the call)

  • 5–10 minute debrief after every call
  • Focus: pacing, multi-threading, pricing conversation, decision-makers, what to tighten in the deck

5. Brandastic application

AMs (Stacy & Maxwell) own relationship + white-space flags on the book.

Digital (Samantha + team) owns channel results that make “happy + results first” true.

Melissa / sales own reopen and referral conversion to opportunities.
