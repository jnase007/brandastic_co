How to Price Your Services

Course pricing guidance lives primarily in the Pitch & Pricing Templates PDF and segment anchors from the sales process sheet:

  • SMB: ~$5k single service anchor (their definition: weak site, small org, thin digital foundation)
  • Mid-Market: ~$10k single service (good brand, digital foundation, budget confirmed)
  • Enterprise: ~$15k+ single service (known brand, large digital org, big spend)

These are their anchors for involvement and packaging — Brandastic must rewrite to our rate card ($150–$175/hr target band from Goals) and retainers.

Brandastic pricing rules

  • Price from value + delivery cost, not fear.
  • If buyer says retainer is unapprovable, sell higher in the org (objection sheet) — do not silently discount first.
  • Keep paid+SEO paired where possible (Video 02 rule).
  • Document floor prices; Justin approves exceptions.
