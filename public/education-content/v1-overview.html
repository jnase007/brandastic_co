Marketing Strategies That Drive Bookings

Full detail hub for Video 01. Not a skim. Use the child pages for execution.

Core problem they solved: Ads scaled pipeline (up to ~$270K/mo) but at scale churn math demanded enormous bookings just to break even. They cut ads and reinvested into a bridge: events + customer marketing + content/SEO team — without becoming “no marketing.”

The three bets

  1. Events — monthly private experiential nights (~$8.5K), 50/50 customers/prospects, plus-ones, no pitch. Open events runbook
  2. Customer marketing — referrals, triggers, job-change gifts, white-space, honest loss reviews. Open customer runbook · Gifting
  3. Content / SEO — 8 pieces per service/sub-service/category; $500 employee side-pay; hubs + internal links. Open SEO 8-piece runbook

Team operating system

  • Thursday written updates (what / why / next) in public channel
  • Autonomy + hard standards; feedback after ship
  • Leadership stays in craft details (ad copy length, hooks, quality of inputs)
