Client gifting system (their exact play)

They called this one of the most successful growth behaviors they run. It is not a branded swag blast. It is fast, personal, low-friction love on clients.

1. The operating rules

  • No approval required for normal client-love gifts
  • Strategist submits the need (“Susie had a migraine”) → ops person sends the gift
  • Keep gifts under ~$100
  • Personal beats expensive (team jersey, something they care about)
  • Intake form + care > budget theater
  • Do not over-track or it stops feeling real
  • Still close the loop: share the thank-you back to the sender (“haptic feedback”)

2. How it connects to bookings

Gifting sits inside the broader customer marketing / relationship engine:

  • Past POCs + referrals called out as a major booking source (example: ~$7M bookings last year via referrals + returning contacts)
  • Recent hard push (~30 days): 4 meetings booked from that motion
  • Trigger alerts: job changes, capital raises, buying signals → act fast
  • Job-change champagne: when a contact leaves for a new role, congratulate + gift → often opens “audit my new agency” conversations
  • Team bonuses for referrals
  • Culture: stay kind and active with people even when the founder is not day-to-day on the account

3. Brandastic application

Step A — Name the ops sender (Sandy’s world / coordinator support). Strategists and AMs request; one person ships.

Step B — Simple intake form: client name, what happened, gift idea, address, who should get the thank-you loop.

Step C — Hard rules: under $100 default; personal; no committee approval under the cap; no public “gift scoreboard” that kills sincerity.

Step D — Trigger list in CRM: job changes, wins, hard months, new baby, new role — AMs (Stacy/Maxwell) and digital leads can flag.

Step E — Close the loop every time: photo/note of thanks back to the person who requested the gift.
