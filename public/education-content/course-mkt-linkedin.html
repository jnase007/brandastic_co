LinkedIn GTM — runbook

This is the operating system to turn LinkedIn into pipeline: research → posts → magnets → qualify → follow-up → meetings. Fill the blanks. If a box is empty, you are not done.

Owner: Justin (offer + founder voice) · Chip (research, drafts, tracking) · whoever owns DMs/sales follow-up. Cadence: 30+ posts / month on the primary exec account. Do not put fake view or revenue totals on this page.

0. Before you write one post

Account + offer lock (30 min)

  1. Primary LinkedIn URL: _______________________
  2. Company we are selling: _______________________
  3. Offer in one sentence (what they buy, for whom, outcome): _______________________
  4. Meeting we want (15-min / audit / demo): _______________________
  5. Booking link: _______________________
  6. CRM / sheet for leads: _______________________
  7. Slack channel for “hot lead”: _______________________

ICP — fill all six or stop

Write 3 buying titles, not “marketers.” Example: Founder, CRO, Head of Growth.

  1. Titles we close: 1) _______ 2) _______ 3) _______
  2. Company size (employees or revenue): _______________________
  3. Industry / niche: _______________________
  4. Trigger (why they care this quarter): _______________________
  5. Objection we hear most: _______________________
  6. Proof we can name (client + result, real numbers only): _______________________

1. Research the market (week 0, then every Monday)

Build a 20–30 name list

Same ICP: competitors, creators they follow, adjacent companies. Sheet columns:

  • Name · LinkedIn URL · Type (competitor / creator / adjacent) · Best post last 30 days · Hook in 8 words · Topic · Why it worked (idea, not the hook) · Steal / ignore

How: Open their LinkedIn. Sort by Top or scan last 20 posts. Copy the 3 posts with the most comments from real titles (not “Great post”). Chip can help scrape/list; a human still picks the idea.

Monday 45 min: Add 5 new outliers. Kill 5 that went quiet. Do not copy hooks. Write the idea (e.g. “show the SOP, not the win”).

Idea bank (always 20 unused ideas)

Each row: Idea · Post type (see §3) · Proof source (call / case / experiment) · Status (draft / scheduled / posted).

If the bank drops under 10, pause publishing and refill from sales calls.

2. Content strategy for the people you want to close

Every post must answer

  1. Who is this for? (one title)
  2. What problem or trigger?
  3. What should they do next? (comment keyword / DM / book / save)
  4. Why would a stranger trust this? (number, screenshot, named example — real only)

If you cannot answer those four, it is a vanity post. Delete it.

3. 30+ posts per month — four buckets

Default mix for one founder account (adjust after 2 weeks of data):

  • 12 distribution — reach new people (patterns, myths, lists, carousels)
  • 8 authority — proof, cases, teardown, “here’s what we did”
  • 6 lead magnets — comment KEYWORD to get the asset
  • 4 conversion — offer, who it’s for, how the engagement works, book the call

Calendar (copy into a sheet)

Rows = days 1–30. Columns: Date · Bucket · Title · CTA · Asset link · Posted? · Impressions · Comments · ICP comments · Magnet requests · Meetings from this post.

Batch write one week on Friday. Schedule the next 7. Never go live with 0 drafts in the bank.

How to write each bucket (do not skip examples)

Distribution: One pattern. 5–8 lines. No pitch. End with a question that ICP can answer in 5 words.

Authority: Situation → what we tried → number → what we’d do again. One client, one metric.

Magnet: Name the asset. Who it’s for. 3 bullets of what’s inside. Visual of the cover. CTA: “Comment PLAYBOOK and I’ll send it.”

Conversion: Who we help / who we don’t. What happens on the call. Link in comments or Featured. No fake urgency.

4. Turn internal knowledge into posts competitors cannot copy

Harvest every week (60 min)

  1. Pull last week’s Fathom / call notes / Slack wins.
  2. Highlight 5 moments: a number, a quote, a failure, a workflow, a founder opinion.
  3. Each moment → one post draft in the idea bank.
  4. Founder records a 10-min voice note answering: “What did a client almost get wrong this week?” Chip turns it into 2 posts.

If there were no sales calls, use: onboarding checklist, QA bug, ad account screenshot (redact), hiring lesson.

5. Eight lead magnets a month (start with 2 if you are not staffed)

Minimum viable magnet (one afternoon)

  1. Pick one ICP problem from §0.
  2. Make a 4–8 page Google Doc or Notion: steps, checklist, 1 example.
  3. Export PDF. Name it like a product: “B2B LinkedIn 30-day posting sheet.”
  4. One image: title + 4 bullets of what’s inside.
  5. Landing or Typeform: Name, work email, company, title, “how can we help.”
  6. Store file in Drive. Link only after they submit, or send PDF in DM.

Month 1 target: 2 magnets. Month 2: 4. Do not promise 8 until Chip + a designer have a slot on the calendar.

Ideas if stuck: posting calendar sheet · ICP comment-qualifier checklist · profile teardown scorecard · first 10 outbound messages · meeting agenda for a 15-min audit.

6. Magnet funnel (manual first, then automate)

Week 1 — human DMs (do this before any tool)

  1. Post magnet. CTA keyword: one word, caps (PLAYBOOK).
  2. Every comment with that word: DM within 2 hours.
  3. DM 1: “Sending it — reply with work email if you want it in inbox too.”
  4. DM 2: PDF or link. One question: “Are you hiring for this in the next 90 days?”
  5. Log in the sheet: Name · URL · Title · Company · ICP? Y/N · Magnet · Next step.

When volume > 20 comments/day: add a tool that sends the first DM + form. Brandastic default: keep the sheet + Slack ping until a tool is chosen. Do not wait on a tool to start.

7. Qualify. Comments ≠ customers

ICP score (2 minutes per person)

Score 0–2 each: title match · company size · industry · buying question in comments/DMs. 6+ = hot. 3–5 = nurture. 0–2 = ignore for sales.

Hot only goes to Slack. Everyone else stays in the magnet list for email.

8. Slack / Telegram ping (copy this)

HOT LinkedIn
Name:
Title:
Company:
LinkedIn:
Magnet they asked for:
Why they scored hot:
Suggested next step (DM / email / book):

Channel: the one you wrote in §0. If sales does not reply same day, Chip follows up once.

9. Warm outbound (not cold spray)

Only message people who already raised a hand

Signals: commented on our post, requested magnet, engaged a competitor post we track, viewed profile after a magnet.

Message (after they got the asset):

“Saw you grabbed the [name] sheet. Most [title]s use it when [trigger]. If useful, I can show how [client type] ran the first 30 days — 15 min, no pitch deck. Open Thursday or Friday?”

Change the bracket to the post they touched. Do not send the same blob to a founder-story engager and a RevOps magnet requester.

Cap: 10 personalized notes / weekday. Log in the sheet.

10. Profile = landing page (do this today)

  1. Headline: Title | who you help | outcome. Not “CEO at Brandastic.”
  2. Banner: offer + CTA + visual of a result (real).
  3. Featured: booking link, best case, best magnet.
  4. About: 1st person. Problem → how we work → who it’s for → CTA.
  5. Featured case: one number, one name you’re allowed to use.
  6. Open-to / services: only if it matches the offer.
  7. Test: open profile incognito. Can a CRO tell what to do in 10 seconds? If not, rewrite headline + Featured.

11. Email for people not ready to book

5 emails, 10 days, to magnet emails only:

  1. Day 0: PDF + one sentence how to use page 1.
  2. Day 2: One case (real).
  3. Day 5: One mistake we see on LinkedIn in their industry.
  4. Day 8: Invite to 15-min audit. Booking link.
  5. Day 10: “Not now is fine — reply STOP. If timing changes, same link.”

Unsub honored. No daily drip.

12. Weekly scorecard (Friday, 20 min)

Track for the primary account:

  • Posts shipped (goal 7–8)
  • Impressions · profile views · followers added
  • Magnet comments · ICP comments · DMs sent · forms · meetings booked · meetings held
  • Pipeline $ if Score Card has it

Kill or rewrite any bucket that got impressions but 0 ICP comments for 2 weeks. A 10k case study that books 1 meeting beats a 500k fluff post.

13. Second exec account (only after 4 weeks of scorecard)

Do not clone the founder’s posts onto the CRO. Same research bank, different ICP:

  • CEO → founders / owners
  • CRO → revenue leaders
  • Growth → marketers

Same sheet, extra column: Account. Same Friday review.

14. Recycle winners

If a post hits ICP comments or a meeting: same week turn it into (pick 2) newsletter blurb · sales one-pager · magnet add-on · outbound first line · short video talking the same example.

15. Monthly review (last Friday)

  1. Which 3 topics got decision-makers?
  2. Which magnet booked meetings?
  3. Which posts were noise?
  4. What goes in next month’s 30-slot calendar?
  5. Update ICP if we keep attracting the wrong titles.

First 7 days (do in order)

  1. Day 1: Fill §0 and §10. Screenshot the profile after.
  2. Day 2: 20-name research list. 10 ideas in the bank.
  3. Day 3: One magnet PDF + image. Form fields live.
  4. Day 4: Write 7 posts (mix of 4 buckets). Founder edits voice.
  5. Day 5: Publish 1 distribution + 1 magnet. Chip on DMs for 48h.
  6. Day 6: Warm notes to ICP commenters. Slack ping template in the channel.
  7. Day 7: Scorecard row 1. Book next week’s writing block on the calendar.
