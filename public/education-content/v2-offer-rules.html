Service offering rules (hard)

  1. Only offer a service if you could win the best RFP in that category.
  2. Never offer a service you can only cross-sell — no gross-profit path; you run out of attach accounts.
  3. If you offer SEO, offer paid media (paired demand). They launched paid around ~$1.5–3M scale.
  4. Refuse scatter work you are not expert in (“can you just do email?”) even when a client asks.
  5. Use MVD thinking: some functions stay small P&L but are required infrastructure (revops example).
