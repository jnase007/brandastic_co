Advertising principles

  • Credential the channel with real spend learning — then rebuild when CAC math breaks
  • Sustainable budget > heroic budget
  • Creative reflects real delivery team, not stock agency clichés
  • Integrate thought leadership with lead gen for price elasticity
  • Personal brand ads for practitioners who host
  • Live interaction (polls) beats pure on-demand when monetizing attention
  • Pair ads with CRM cadence (Salesforce campaign + HubSpot workflow + calendar reminders)
