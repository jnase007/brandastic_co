How to Write Contracts

Primary course artifact: Paid Media Project + Ongoing Management SOW.

From the sales process, SOW is Stage 4 — built after ROI/decision criteria, sent with price, followed by Trade meeting with final decision makers.

What a complete SOW pack should include

  • Scope phases (project vs ongoing)
  • Deliverables and cadence
  • Client responsibilities
  • Fees, payment terms, start date
  • Term, termination, notice
  • Assumptions and out-of-scope
  • Signatures

Brandastic tickets

  • Clone Paid Media SOW into SEO, Web, Creative versions.
  • Legal/RJI pass on terms once, then lock templates.
  • CRM checklist: no “closed won” without signed SOW + kickoff date.
