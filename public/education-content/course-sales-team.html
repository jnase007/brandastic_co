How to Build a Sales Team

Hiring bar for Account Executives from their AE scorecard — subject-matter expertise, proposal skill, goals, follow-up, tech, and sales development.

We are looking for an AE who has strong subject matter expertise in not only performance marketing, but also, SaaS. Ideally, this indiviual is deeply skilled at prospecting, sales development, building presentations, pitching, negotiating, and winning new business.

SME for Performance Marketing

How would you rank yourself 1-10 on Paid Media, SEO, MarketingOps, Creative, and Strategy? Have you ever done the work? What campaigns did you work on (and what exactly did you do?) and what was the size of the engagement?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Proposal Building and Presentation Skills

What do you believe goes into a great presentation? With access to a clients information, can you build their strategy yourself or would you need support from operations?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Hitting goals

Can you tell me about a time that you missed a number, what did you do to fix that?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Follow up

Can you tell me about a time that you won a deal that no one thought you could and what your followup process was like?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Technology

What's your experience with SF, Gong, Outreach, HS?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Sales Development

Have you ever done sales development? How do you plan on making sure you always hit your proposals given number here at Directive?

Rate 1–10 + comments in interview.

Brandastic use

Use this scorecard when hiring or leveling sales talent. Pair with org reality: Melissa/Erin path + Justin final offer approval. Do not hire AE who cannot build strategy with client data.
