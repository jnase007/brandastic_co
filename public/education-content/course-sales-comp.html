How to Set Quotas and Compensation Plans

Compensation artifacts from Agency Academy are hosted as downloadable templates. This page tells you what they are and how Brandastic should use them.

Files on the hub

  • AE Compensation Plan template (2023)
  • SME Compensation Plan template (2023)
  • VP of Sales role doc

How to use (process)

  1. Open AE + SME templates side by side.
  2. Extract: base, variable, quota definition, accelerators, spiffs, clawbacks, what counts as booking vs revenue.
  3. Rewrite into Brandastic dollars and roles (we may not have identical AE/SME titles — map to Melissa path + any future closer).
  4. Align with Finance (RJI / Sandy) before publishing.
  5. Put final plan in offer letters and quarterly review packets.
