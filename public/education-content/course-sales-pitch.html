Process from sales stages (Stage 2–5)

  1. Proposal: craft proposal + account plan after strategy call; schedule with decision makers.
  2. ROI + Decision Criteria: build decision criteria with the buyer; review meeting before SOW.
  3. SOW: build SOW; send with price; schedule Trade meeting for final decision makers.
  4. Trade: final negotiation / close path.

Executive involvement rises with segment: SMB light; Mid-Market Innovation Team on proposals; Enterprise Partners/VP/Directors on disco through SOW.
